# KTD-Base12-Engine
**Deterministic Transactional Kernel in Base-12 (Edge AI Core)**

Sovereign edge transactional processing architecture designed under advanced mathematical models for ultra-high resource efficiency (*zero dependencies*) and decentralized mesh topology.

---

## 🏛️ Architecture Overview
The **KTD-Base12-Engine** breaks away from traditional corporate star-topology bottlenecks. Instead of relying on massive queries to centralized cloud databases, the kernel decentralizes validation by executing autonomous processing micro-islands directly at the node edge.

### Core Modules (`src/`)
1. **Geometric Base-12 Classifier (`dodecahedral_classifier.py`)**: Validates strict modular congruence of transactional frames ($\sum (i+1)b_i \pmod{12} \equiv 0$) regulated by the golden ratio ($\phi = 1.618$).
2. **RK4 Predictive Engine (`rk4_predictive_core.py`)**: Models financial derivatives using 4th-order Runge-Kutta to anticipate risk and fraud vectors in real-time.
3. **Thermal Impedance Breaker (`thermal_breaker.py`)**: Evaluates the critical threshold ($Z \ge 16.18$), triggering immediate flow isolation or cutoff at the edge upon severe anomalies.
4. **Immutable Forensic Ledger (`immutable_ledger.py`)**: Guarantees forensic traceability by logging events into a local SHA-256 encrypted blockchain at zero network cost.
5. **Gateway Adapter (`gateway_adapter.py`)**: Unifies the complete pipeline to interact with payment gateways modularly.

---

## 🔒 Security Hardening by Design
* **Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Attacks:** Neutralized via local SHA-256 cryptographic chaining and Base-12 parity. Any single-byte alteration in the frame breaks the harmonic chain and is immediately rejected at the edge.
* **Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS):** Mitigated by decentralized mesh topology and the thermal impedance breaker ($Z \ge 16.18$), autonomously isolating malicious traffic before hardware saturation.
* **Buffer Overflows:** Prevented through an ultra-optimized codebase (under 300 total lines), free of insecure pointers or third-party dependencies, processing strictly bounded and structured data frames.

Storage requirements are exclusively reduced to compact ledger hash logging, radically optimizing operational expenditures (*Cloud Cost Optimization*).
